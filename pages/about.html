About CARE

Because We Care.

Our Mission

To make everyday life easier, safer and happier for elderly people by providing trusted help and a warm community, close to home.

Our Vision

A city where every elderly person feels respected, connected and never alone.

What we offer

🚶 Daily help

Walking, grocery shopping, cooking, household tasks and doctor visits with a trusted companion.

👥 Community

Neighbourhood groups for walking, yoga, games and music, so friendships grow naturally.

🎉 Events

Festivals, workshops and gatherings at our CARE Hubs throughout the year.

💙 CARE Fellows

CARE Fellows are kind, trained local people. They visit homes, assist with daily tasks and become familiar, friendly faces. Each Fellow is matched to people in their own neighbourhood.

🏠 CARE Hubs

CARE Hubs are welcoming community spaces where elderly people meet, join activities, attend events and find support from the CARE team.
